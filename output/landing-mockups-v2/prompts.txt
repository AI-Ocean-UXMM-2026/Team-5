Employers register → team verifies → post challenge → review → interview/offer. Hiring decisions made by companies. No promise of guaranteed employment. Include FAQ, final brand CTA and professional footer. All showcased company work labelled Example challenge, showcased learning content labelled Preview where appropriate. Do not include meta comments, variant number, design labels or mockup annotations inside website. This should feel like an award-worthy but buildable education startup website, substantial visual storytelling with distinct sections, not a flowchart poster.
