Why choose B

  • Feels friendly and low-pressure for 18–30 first-timers; one small question at a time is easy on a phone.
  • Gives Cit, the mascot, a real job instead of decoration, which makes the brand memorable.
  • The "Your answers" card fixes the usual chat problem: every answer stays visible and editable.
  • Translates well: short bubbles work in Burmese without cramped layouts.

Trade-offs

  • More taps for people who just want a form; offer "fill in a form instead" for companies.
  • Chat UIs need care for screen readers (announce new messages) and the Back button.
  • Can feel slow on desktop if the bubble animations are long; keep them under 300 ms.

Build effort

  • Medium. Same data and steps, but a new chat component and per-question state.
