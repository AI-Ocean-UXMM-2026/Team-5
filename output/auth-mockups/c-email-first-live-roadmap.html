Why choose C

  • Removes the most common auth mistake: people with an account pressing "Sign up" (and the reverse). One box handles both.
  • Showing the roadmap while people answer proves the value early, which keeps them going to the end.
  • Free vs paid is visible on every course from the first minute, so there are no surprises later.
  • Matches the doc's rule that AI suggests and the learner accepts: the preview is clearly "not saved yet".

Trade-offs

  • Telling people whether an email has an account can leak that it's registered; a real build should rate-limit this check.
  • The live preview needs the roadmap to be quick to recompute; fine for today's rule-based demo, needs care with a real AI call.
  • Two-pane layout needs a different pattern on phones (the bottom sheet).

Build effort

  • Medium to high. New auth page logic plus a reactive preview panel.
