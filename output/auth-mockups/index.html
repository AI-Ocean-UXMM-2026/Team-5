The Citadel · Sign up, log in and onboarding

Three design directions

All three follow the corrected flow in docs/citadel-flow-and-ia.md: choose role, accept terms (18+), confirm email with a code, then language, goal (or "not sure yet"), roadmap review and an optional profile. They differ in how the screens work, not only in looks.

MY RECOMMENDATION

C's structure, with A's controls and B's voice

  • Auth: C. One "Start here" email box removes the sign up vs log in confusion, and there's no password to forget.
  • Onboarding: C's live roadmap with A's goal cards, skill chips and hours selector, which are faster than typing on a phone.
  • Microcopy: B's friendly tone, with Cit appearing at key moments (welcome, code sent, roadmap ready) instead of a full chat.
