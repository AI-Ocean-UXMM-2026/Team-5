Why choose A

  • Most familiar pattern, so it's the lowest risk and quickest to build from today's code.
  • The trust points (free to start, verified companies) sit next to the form, right when people decide to sign up.
  • The step rail answers "how long is this?" and "where am I?" on every onboarding screen.
  • Chips and an hours selector are faster than typing on a phone, and give the AI cleaner input.

Trade-offs

  • Looks like many other products; the personality comes mostly from the artwork.
  • On phones the left panel shrinks to a banner, so the trust points move below the form.

Build effort

  • Low. Same screens and steps as now, new layout and controls.
